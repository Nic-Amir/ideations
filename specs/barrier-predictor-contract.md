# barrier-predictor-contract Specification

## Purpose

Defines the Barrier Predictor contract lifecycle: the two-sided barrier
selection (`UPPER`/`LOWER`), first-to-touch resolution against a pair of
log-symmetric barriers around the entry spot, the four-state status
machine (`OPEN` → `{WON,LOST,REFUNDED}`), barrier and parameter locking,
path generation and audit replay, player-chosen tick duration, stake
bounds, the one-active-contract constraint, and the backend-owned
trading principle. Probability and payout maths are delegated to
`barrier-predictor-pricing`; money movement and ledger mechanics to
`barrier-predictor-settlement`.

Barrier Predictor is a first-passage corridor product. A single
synthetic index evolves as driftless Geometric Brownian Motion from an
entry spot `S₀`, bounded by an upper barrier `U = S₀ × exp(+d)` and a
lower barrier `L = S₀ × exp(−d)`. The player predicts which barrier the
price will touch first within `T` ticks. If neither barrier is touched
by maturity, the stake is refunded in full. Unlike `rise-fall-contract`,
settlement is on first touch rather than at a fixed expiry comparison.
Unlike `barrier-race-contract`, there is one asset and two barriers, not
two assets racing to one barrier.

The tick path is generated by the backend from a seeded cryptographic
RNG at `/buy` acceptance (the same server-authoritative pattern as
`barrier-race-contract`). There is no deferred barrier-locking-from-tick
semantics: `S₀`, `U`, and `L` are known and locked at placement.

## Requirements
### Requirement: Barrier Selection

A Barrier Predictor contract SHALL carry exactly one `selection`, naming which barrier the player predicts the price will touch first:

- **UPPER** — wins when the upper barrier `U` is touched before the lower barrier `L`, within `tick_duration` ticks.
- **LOWER** — wins when the lower barrier `L` is touched before the upper barrier `U`, within `tick_duration` ticks.

The contract is WON when the selected barrier is the first-touched barrier. It is LOST when the opposite barrier is touched first. It is REFUNDED when neither barrier is touched by tick `T` (`settle_reason = "no_touch"`).

Unlike `rise-fall-contract` where REFUNDED is reachable only via operational paths, `no_touch` is an ordinary contract outcome here and pays `stake × 1.0`.

The selection is locked at placement and stored verbatim on the contract row in `parameters.selection`.

#### Scenario: Selected UPPER touches first → WON

- **WHEN** a contract selects `UPPER` and the price first reaches or exceeds `U` at tick 7 while remaining strictly above `L` on every tick up to and including 7
- **THEN** the contract is WON and the player receives the contract's locked `potential_payout` (per `barrier-predictor-pricing` "Commission Model")

#### Scenario: Selected LOWER touches first → WON

- **WHEN** a contract selects `LOWER` and the price first reaches or falls to `L` at tick 4 while remaining strictly below `U` on every tick up to and including 4
- **THEN** the contract is WON and the player receives the contract's locked `potential_payout`

#### Scenario: Opposite barrier touches first → LOST

- **WHEN** a contract selects `UPPER` and the price first reaches or falls to `L` at tick 8
- **THEN** the contract is LOST and the player receives nothing (the stake paid at placement remains with the house)

#### Scenario: Neither barrier touched by maturity → REFUNDED

- **WHEN** the contract reaches tick `T = tick_duration` with every observed price strictly inside `(L, U)`
- **THEN** the contract is REFUNDED with `settle_reason = "no_touch"` regardless of the player's selection
- **AND** the stake is credited back via a REFUND ledger row (`amount = +stake`)

### Requirement: First-to-Touch Resolution

The contract outcome MUST be determined by discrete-time first-passage against the locked pair of barriers. After each generated tick price `S_t` for `t ∈ {1, 2, …, T}`:

1. If `S_t ≥ U` (compared at 1e-8 precision): the upper barrier is touched → settle immediately.
2. Else if `S_t ≤ L` (compared at 1e-8 precision): the lower barrier is touched → settle immediately.
3. Else if `t = T`: maturity reached → REFUNDED with `settle_reason = "no_touch"`.
4. Otherwise: continue to the next tick.

Upper is checked **before** lower. A same-tick dual touch (both `S_t ≥ U` and `S_t ≤ L`) is vanishingly rare under continuous GBM with `U > L`, and is resolved as an upper touch by this evaluation order. There is no `tie` refund reason.

An exact-barrier value counts as a touch. Prices are observed **only at integer tick boundaries**. The backend MUST NOT apply continuous-time monitoring: a path whose continuous-time trajectory would have crossed a barrier between two tick boundaries but whose observed prices at both boundaries are strictly inside `(L, U)` has NOT touched.

The contract terminates at `settle_tick_index = τ` when a barrier is first touched, or at `T` on no-touch. No ticks after the settling tick are generated, revealed, or persisted.

#### Scenario: Exact upper barrier counts as a touch

- **WHEN** the observed price at tick 5 equals `U` exactly (within 1e-8) and every prior tick was strictly inside `(L, U)`
- **THEN** the upper barrier has been touched and `settle_tick_index = 5`

#### Scenario: Exact lower barrier counts as a touch

- **WHEN** the observed price at tick 3 equals `L` exactly (within 1e-8) and every prior tick was strictly inside `(L, U)`
- **THEN** the lower barrier has been touched and `settle_tick_index = 3`

#### Scenario: Intra-tick excursion is not a touch

- **WHEN** the observed price is strictly inside `(L, U)` at tick 5 and again at tick 6
- **THEN** neither barrier has been touched on tick 5 or tick 6, irrespective of any notional continuous-time path between the two observations

#### Scenario: Upper-before-lower evaluation order

- **WHEN** a single tick price somehow satisfies both `S_t ≥ U` and `S_t ≤ L`
- **THEN** the contract settles as an upper touch
- **AND** there is no `tie` `settle_reason`

#### Scenario: Contract terminates on the first touch

- **WHEN** the upper barrier is first touched at tick 7
- **THEN** `settle_tick_index = 7`
- **AND** no tick with index greater than 7 is generated or revealed for this contract

### Requirement: Contract Lifecycle

A contract MUST progress through a four-state status machine:

1. `OPEN` — from `/buy` acceptance until terminal. The full tick path is generated at acceptance (per "Path Generation and Auditability"), so the outcome is determined at acceptance; the contract stays `OPEN` while the path is progressively revealed to the player tick by tick. Within `OPEN` there are two observable sub-states distinguished by the `reveal_start_at` column:
   - **Awaiting reveal** (`reveal_start_at IS NULL`): from `/buy` acceptance until the reveal stream begins.
   - **Running** (`reveal_start_at IS NOT NULL`): from the first revealed tick until the settlement trigger fires at `settle_tick_index`.
2. One terminal state: `WON`, `LOST`, or `REFUNDED`.

A contract MUST NOT transition out of a terminal state. The status enum is exactly `{OPEN, WON, LOST, REFUNDED}`.

REFUNDED contracts are distinguished by `settle_reason` (admin-only column per `barrier-predictor-database` "contracts Table"), which takes one of two values:

- `"no_touch"` — neither barrier was touched within `T` ticks. An ordinary contract outcome. `settle_tick_index = T`.
- `"operator_refund"` — an operator or automated monitoring voided the contract (for example, a discovered path-generation defect or a config published in error). The only non-outcome refund path. `settle_tick_index` MAY be NULL if the contract was voided before resolution.

Because the path is generated in full at acceptance, a process restart does not lose the outcome: recovery re-reads the persisted seed and locked parameters, replays the path deterministically, and resumes or completes settlement (per `barrier-predictor-settlement` "Process Lifecycle and Recovery"). There is no process-lifecycle refund reason.

#### Scenario: Reveal begins while OPEN

- **WHEN** a contract is `OPEN` with `reveal_start_at IS NULL` and the reveal stream emits its first tick
- **THEN** the contract stays `OPEN`
- **AND** `reveal_start_at` is set to that instant

#### Scenario: OPEN → terminal on settlement trigger

- **WHEN** the reveal reaches `settle_tick_index` for an `OPEN` contract
- **THEN** the contract transitions to one of `WON`, `LOST`, or `REFUNDED` per "Barrier Selection"

#### Scenario: Terminal state is final

- **WHEN** a contract has status `WON`, `LOST`, or `REFUNDED`
- **THEN** no subsequent event transitions it to any other status
- **AND** re-running settlement for that contract is a no-op

#### Scenario: Restart replays the path rather than refunding

- **WHEN** the backend restarts while a contract is `OPEN` and the contract's `settle_tick_index` was reached during the downtime window
- **THEN** recovery replays the path from the persisted seed and locked parameters and settles the contract to its determined outcome
- **AND** the contract is NOT refunded on account of the restart

### Requirement: Contract Timestamps

Every contract MUST carry the following timing fields, timestamps as RFC 3339 values:

| Field | Meaning | NULL when |
|---|---|---|
| `start_time` | The instant the backend received the `/buy` request. The instant at which `S₀`, barriers, duration, and probabilities are locked. | never |
| `tick_duration` | The player-chosen maximum number of ticks `T`. Locked at INSERT. Not a timestamp. | never |
| `reveal_start_at` | The instant the reveal stream emitted the contract's first post-entry tick. | `OPEN`-while-awaiting-reveal; `operator_refund` voided before reveal began |
| `settle_tick_index` | The integer tick index at which the contract resolved: the first-touch tick for WON / LOST, `T` for `no_touch`. Not a timestamp. | `operator_refund` voided before resolution |
| `settle_tick_at` | The instant the resolving tick was revealed and the contract was settled. | non-terminal; `operator_refund` voided before resolution |

Invariants:

- For every terminal contract other than a pre-resolution `operator_refund`: `start_time ≤ reveal_start_at < settle_tick_at`.
- `settle_tick_index ≥ 1` for WON and LOST contracts — the earliest possible touch is tick 1.
- For `no_touch` REFUNDED contracts: `settle_tick_index = tick_duration`.
- Unlike `rise-fall-contract`, a `no_touch` REFUNDED Barrier Predictor contract DOES carry a resolving tick index: maturity is an outcome of a completed round, not the absence of a resolution. Only `operator_refund` may leave `settle_tick_index` and `settle_tick_at` NULL.
- `settle_tick_index` is derived from the path and MUST be reproducible by replaying the persisted seed against the locked parameters. `settle_tick_at` is wall-clock and is NOT reproducible.
- The reveal cadence is a presentation concern (per `barrier-predictor-game-ui`) and MAY differ from one tick per second; `settle_tick_at − reveal_start_at` need not equal `settle_tick_index` seconds.

#### Scenario: tick_duration locked at placement

- **WHEN** a placement with `tick_duration = 10` is accepted at `start_time = T0`
- **THEN** the row's `tick_duration = 10` is stored at INSERT
- **AND** `tick_duration` is never updated after INSERT

#### Scenario: Won contract carries a resolving tick index

- **WHEN** a contract settles WON because the selected barrier was touched at tick 7
- **THEN** `settle_tick_index = 7`
- **AND** `settle_tick_at` is non-null and satisfies `reveal_start_at < settle_tick_at`

#### Scenario: No-touch refund pins the index at T

- **WHEN** a contract with `tick_duration = 10` is REFUNDED because neither barrier was touched
- **THEN** `settle_reason = "no_touch"`
- **AND** `settle_tick_index = 10`

#### Scenario: Operator refund may leave tick fields null

- **WHEN** a contract is REFUNDED with `settle_reason = "operator_refund"` before the path has resolved
- **THEN** `settle_tick_index` and `settle_tick_at` MAY be NULL
- **AND** the row is distinguishable from a `no_touch` refund by `settle_reason`

### Requirement: Barrier and Parameter Locking

The following values MUST be locked at `start_time` and MUST NOT change for the life of the contract:

| Parameter | Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `entry_spot` | S₀ | Spot price at placement; barrier anchor |
| `upper_barrier` | U | `S₀ × exp(+d)` |
| `lower_barrier` | L | `S₀ × exp(−d)` |
| `barrier_offset` | d | Log-distance used to place U and L |
| `tick_duration` | T | Maximum ticks to maturity |
| `instrument` | — | Synthetic index ID (e.g. `V_100`) |
| `volatility` | σ | Instrument annualized volatility at `start_time` |
| `commission_rate` | c | Platform margin at `start_time` |
| `distance_factor` | — | Optional preset scale applied to the calibrated default offset |
| `config_version` | — | Version of the pricing / instrument settings used |

The locked set is persisted together with its pricing outputs in `locked_pricing` (per `barrier-predictor-pricing` "Recorded Pricing Inputs"). Barriers are known before the reveal begins and are quotable on the `/preview` and `/buy` responses — there is no deferred barrier as in `rise-fall-contract` "Barrier Locking".

Log-symmetry is mandatory: `U × L = S₀²` (within decimal precision). Subsequent admin edits to instrument volatility or commission MUST NOT re-price, re-generate the path for, or re-resolve any contract that was already accepted.

#### Scenario: Barriers quotable at placement

- **WHEN** `POST /api/barrier-predictor/buy` succeeds with `entry_spot = "100000.000000"` and `barrier_offset` such that `U` and `L` are computed
- **THEN** the HTTP response body includes `entry_spot`, `upper_barrier`, and `lower_barrier`
- **AND** those values are identical to the values persisted on the contract row

#### Scenario: Log-symmetric barriers

- **WHEN** barriers are computed from `S₀ = 100000` and `d = 0.001`
- **THEN** `upper_barrier = 100000 × exp(0.001)` and `lower_barrier = 100000 × exp(−0.001)`
- **AND** `upper_barrier × lower_barrier = S₀²` (within decimal precision)

#### Scenario: Admin volatility change does not affect an open contract

- **WHEN** an admin updates `V_100` volatility from `1.0` to `0.75` while a contract accepted under the previous settings is `OPEN`
- **THEN** the `OPEN` contract retains its locked `volatility = 1.0`, barriers, and `potential_payout`
- **AND** the contract's already-generated path and determined outcome are unchanged
- **AND** any new placement after the change is priced under the new volatility

### Requirement: Path Generation and Auditability

The backend MUST generate the complete tick path at `/buy` acceptance, before responding, and MUST persist enough state to replay it deterministically.

Path generation follows driftless GBM. With `dt = 1 / (365 × 24 × 3600)` (one second in years) and instrument volatility `σ`:

```
S_t = S_{t−1} × exp(−½ σ² dt + σ √dt × Z_t)
```

where `Z_t ~ N(0,1)` and `S_0 = entry_spot`. After each tick the first-to-touch rules in "First-to-Touch Resolution" are applied; generation stops at the resolving tick.

Requirements on the generator:

- The seed MUST be drawn from a cryptographically secure random source. Non-cryptographic PRNGs MUST NOT be used to generate a path that determines money movement.
- The seed MUST be persisted on the contract row as an admin-only column (per `barrier-predictor-database` "contracts Table"). It MUST NOT be exposed on player-facing endpoints or WebSocket envelopes before the contract is terminal, because the seed reveals the outcome.
- The pair `(seed, locked parameters)` MUST fully determine the path. Replaying it MUST reproduce every observed price, the touch side (if any), `settle_tick_index`, and the outcome, bit-for-bit.
- The path MUST NOT be regenerated, extended, or modified after acceptance.
- Generation MUST stop at the resolving tick: no prices beyond `settle_tick_index` are generated or persisted.

#### Scenario: Path generated before the buy response

- **WHEN** `POST /api/barrier-predictor/buy` is accepted
- **THEN** the full path, touch side (if any), `settle_tick_index`, and the outcome are computed and persisted before the HTTP response is written
- **AND** no later event can change them

#### Scenario: Replay reproduces the outcome

- **WHEN** an auditor replays a settled contract's `(seed, locked parameters)`
- **THEN** the regenerated price series matches the persisted series exactly
- **AND** the regenerated `settle_tick_index` and outcome match the persisted values

#### Scenario: Seed not exposed while OPEN

- **WHEN** a player reads their own `OPEN` contract via `GET /api/barrier-predictor/contracts/{id}`
- **THEN** the response does NOT include the seed
- **AND** the response does NOT include any path tick beyond those already revealed

#### Scenario: Weak randomness rejected

- **WHEN** a path generator is implemented against a non-cryptographic PRNG or a caller-supplied seed
- **THEN** the implementation violates this requirement and MUST NOT be deployed

### Requirement: Duration Bounds

The `tick_duration` on the wire MUST be an integer in the closed range `[1, 20]`. The default when omitted is `10`. Each tick represents one second of simulated GBM time (`dt = 1 / (365 × 24 × 3600)` years).

A `tick_duration` outside `[1, 20]` is rejected as `invalid_duration` at parse / validation time. The duration is locked at placement and stored in `parameters.tick_duration`.

Unlike `barrier-race-contract`, duration is a player-selectable input: shorter `T` yields a shorter corridor walk and a different calibrated default `d` (per `barrier-predictor-pricing` "Default Offset Calibration").

#### Scenario: Default duration accepted

- **WHEN** a placement omits `tick_duration`
- **THEN** the placement is accepted with `tick_duration = 10`

#### Scenario: Within-range duration accepted

- **WHEN** a placement specifies `tick_duration = 15`
- **THEN** the placement is accepted
- **AND** `parameters.tick_duration = 15`

#### Scenario: Duration below floor rejected

- **WHEN** a placement specifies `tick_duration = 0`
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `INVALID_DURATION` (specifically `invalid_duration`)

#### Scenario: Duration above ceiling rejected

- **WHEN** a placement specifies `tick_duration = 21`
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `INVALID_DURATION` (specifically `invalid_duration`)

#### Scenario: Duration stored verbatim

- **WHEN** a contract is accepted with `tick_duration = 5`
- **THEN** the contract row's `parameters.tick_duration` is the integer `5`

### Requirement: Barrier Offset Bounds

The `barrier_offset` `d` is the log-distance used to place the barriers. It MUST be strictly positive. The default `d` is **auto-calibrated** by `barrier-predictor-pricing` "Default Offset Calibration" so that the discrete `P(touch) ≈ 0.5` for the chosen `tick_duration` (and instrument `σ`). A player MAY scale the calibrated default via a distance factor (Near `0.75`, Standard `1.0`, Far `1.4`) or submit an explicit positive `d` within the pricing-supported range.

A non-positive `d`, a sub-precision offset outside the pricing CDF / grid's supported range, or an offset that fails `barrier-predictor-pricing` "Probability Clamp and Offered-Odds Viability" is rejected with `unsupported_barrier_offset` (HTTP 422).

The locked `barrier_offset`, `upper_barrier`, and `lower_barrier` are exposed on player endpoints and WebSocket envelopes as producer-precision decimal strings.

#### Scenario: Auto-calibrated default accepted

- **WHEN** a placement on `V_100` with `tick_duration = 10` omits `barrier_offset` (Standard distance)
- **THEN** the placement is accepted
- **AND** the locked `d` is the discrete calibration that yields `P(touch) ≈ 0.5` for `T = 10`
- **AND** the locked `offered_odds` is approximately `"1.88"` (per `barrier-predictor-pricing` "Commission Model")

#### Scenario: Near distance factor accepted

- **WHEN** a placement uses distance factor `0.75` (Near)
- **THEN** the locked `d` is `0.75 ×` the calibrated default for that `T`
- **AND** `P(touch)` is greater than at Standard, and the offered multiplier is richer

#### Scenario: Far distance factor accepted

- **WHEN** a placement uses distance factor `1.4` (Far)
- **THEN** the locked `d` is `1.4 ×` the calibrated default for that `T`
- **AND** `P(touch)` is less than at Standard, and the offered multiplier is leaner

#### Scenario: Non-positive offset rejected

- **WHEN** a placement carries `barrier_offset = "0"` or a negative value
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with HTTP 422 `unsupported_barrier_offset`

### Requirement: Stake Bounds

The `stake` (USDT) on the wire MUST be a 6-decimal-place decimal string whose value is strictly positive and falls within `[min_stake, max_stake]` for the active game settings (defaults per product configuration: min `0.10`, max `100.00`, subject to live settings per `barrier-predictor-risk`).

The stake is locked at placement and debited verbatim. The contract row stores `stake` in `parameters.stake` (JSONB), and stores the same value as the top-level NUMERIC column `buy_price` (per `barrier-predictor-database` "contracts Table" and `barrier-predictor-pricing` "Commission Model").

Monetary arithmetic MUST use a decimal-safe representation (`NUMERIC(20,6)` in Postgres, `shopspring/decimal` in Go). Whole-credit or `float64` money handling is forbidden.

A placement whose `stake × offered_odds` would exceed the configured `max_single_payout` MUST be rejected at the risk cascade (per `barrier-predictor-risk` "Per-Contract Max Payout").

#### Scenario: Zero or negative stake rejected

- **WHEN** a placement carries `stake = "0.000000"` or any negative value
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `INVALID_STAKE`

#### Scenario: Stake below configured minimum

- **WHEN** the placement's stake is below the config's `min_stake`
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `INVALID_STAKE` (specifically `stake_below_min`)

#### Scenario: Stake above configured maximum

- **WHEN** the placement's stake is above the config's `max_stake`
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `INVALID_STAKE` (specifically `stake_above_max`)

#### Scenario: Stake stored verbatim in two places

- **WHEN** a placement with `stake = "10.000000"` is accepted
- **THEN** the contract row's `parameters.stake` is the exact string `"10.000000"`
- **AND** the row's `buy_price` NUMERIC column equals `10.000000`
- **AND** the PLAY_DEBIT ledger row has `amount = -10.000000`

### Requirement: One Active Contract Per Account

The backend MUST allow an account to hold at most **one** non-terminal Barrier Predictor contract at a time. A placement submitted while the account already holds a non-terminal contract MUST be rejected with `trade_in_progress`.

This matches `barrier-race-contract` "One Active Race Per Account" and product_spec §6.2. A Barrier Predictor round is a single progressively-revealed corridor walk; concurrent contracts would make the reveal surface ambiguous.

The constraint MUST be enforced atomically against the account's contract set so that two placements racing in parallel cannot both be accepted.

#### Scenario: Second concurrent placement rejected

- **WHEN** an account holds an `OPEN` contract and submits a new placement
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `trade_in_progress`
- **AND** no stake is debited and no path is generated

#### Scenario: Placement accepted once the prior contract is terminal

- **WHEN** an account's only contract reaches `WON`, `LOST`, or `REFUNDED` and the account then submits a new placement
- **THEN** the placement is evaluated normally against the remaining risk cascade

#### Scenario: Parallel placements cannot both win

- **WHEN** an account submits two placements simultaneously with distinct idempotency keys and holds no non-terminal contract
- **THEN** exactly one is accepted and the other is rejected with `trade_in_progress`

#### Scenario: Retry of the same idempotency key is not a second trade

- **WHEN** a client retries an accepted placement with the same idempotency key while that contract is `OPEN`
- **THEN** the original contract is returned per `barrier-predictor-api` "Idempotency"
- **AND** the retry is NOT rejected with `trade_in_progress`

### Requirement: Backend-Owned Trading Logic

All trading logic MUST live on the backend, identically for real-money (`ACC-`) and demo (`DEMO-`) accounts:

- Contracts are created exclusively by the backend in response to `POST /api/barrier-predictor/buy`. There is no WebSocket placement path.
- The tick path is generated by the backend (see "Path Generation and Auditability"). The frontend MUST NOT generate, extend, or simulate a path that determines money movement. The client-side path generation used in prototypes is not a permitted production path.
- The win / loss / refund outcome is decided by the backend (see "Barrier Selection" and `barrier-predictor-settlement`).
- Balance debits and credits happen on the backend; the frontend reads via `GET /api/barrier-predictor/balance` and `BALANCE_UPDATE`.
- Contract history is sourced via `GET /api/barrier-predictor/contracts`.

Because the outcome is determined at acceptance rather than by later external events, the reveal surface carries a disclosure obligation: the backend MUST NOT expose any part of the path ahead of the reveal position, and MUST NOT expose the seed or the outcome, until the contract is terminal.

The reveal cadence is a presentation concern owned by `barrier-predictor-game-ui`. It MUST NOT alter `settle_tick_index`, the outcome, or any money movement.

#### Scenario: No client-side bypass

- **WHEN** the frontend handles a placement gesture in any mode (real, demo, or embed)
- **THEN** there is no code path that generates a tick path, decides an outcome, or moves a balance without a corresponding backend response

#### Scenario: Unrevealed path not readable

- **WHEN** a contract is `OPEN` with the reveal at tick 3 of a round resolving at tick 9
- **THEN** no player-facing endpoint or WebSocket envelope exposes prices for ticks 4 through 9
- **AND** no player-facing surface exposes the seed, `settle_tick_index`, or the outcome

#### Scenario: Full path readable once terminal

- **WHEN** a contract has settled
- **THEN** the full revealed price series, `settle_tick_index`, and the outcome are readable by the owning player
- **AND** the seed remains admin-only

#### Scenario: Demo accounts follow the identical path

- **WHEN** a `DEMO-` account places a contract
- **THEN** path generation, outcome determination, and settlement follow the identical backend code path as an `ACC-` account
- **AND** only the account's funding source differs
