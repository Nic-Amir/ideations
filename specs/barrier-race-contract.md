# barrier-race-contract Specification

## Purpose

Defines the Barrier Race contract lifecycle: the two-asset selection
(`DRIFT`/`VOL`), first-to-touch resolution against a shared up-barrier,
the four-state status machine (`OPEN` → `{WON,LOST,REFUNDED}`),
race-parameter locking, path generation and audit replay, the `T_max`
timeout bound, stake bounds, the one-active-race constraint, and the
backend-owned trading principle. Probability and payout maths are
delegated to `barrier-race-pricing`; money movement and ledger
mechanics to `barrier-race-settlement`.

Barrier Race is a first-passage product, not a fixed-expiry product. Two
simulated assets evolve as correlated Geometric Brownian Motions from a
common start price, and the race resolves the instant either asset
touches the barrier. Unlike `rise-fall-contract`, there is no upstream
market feed: both asset paths are generated by the backend from a
seeded cryptographic RNG, so this capability has no `-feed` sibling and
no barrier-locking-from-tick semantics. The barrier is a configuration
value known before placement, not a value derived from a later tick.

## Requirements
### Requirement: Asset Selection

A Barrier Race contract SHALL carry exactly one `selection`, naming which of the two competing assets the player predicts will touch the barrier first:

- **DRIFT** — Asset 1, the high-drift / low-volatility profile (the statistical favourite; reaches the barrier through steady deterministic progression).
- **VOL** — Asset 2, the low-drift / high-volatility profile (the underdog; reaches the barrier through large stochastic jumps).

The contract is WON when the selected asset is the **sole** first-toucher of the barrier. It is LOST when the asset the player did not select is the sole first-toucher. It is REFUNDED when neither asset is a sole first-toucher — either both touched on the same tick (`tie`) or neither touched within `T_max` ticks (`timeout`).

Unlike `rise-fall-contract` where REFUNDED is reachable only via operational paths, `tie` and `timeout` are ordinary race outcomes here. Both pay `stake × 1.0`.

The selection is locked at placement and stored verbatim on the contract row in `parameters.selection`.

#### Scenario: Selected DRIFT touches first → WON

- **WHEN** a contract selects `DRIFT` and Asset 1 touches the barrier at tick 7 while Asset 2 has not touched at or before tick 7
- **THEN** the contract is WON and the player receives the contract's locked `potential_payout` (per `barrier-race-pricing` "Commission Model")

#### Scenario: Selected VOL touches first → WON

- **WHEN** a contract selects `VOL` and Asset 2 touches the barrier at tick 4 while Asset 1 has not touched at or before tick 4
- **THEN** the contract is WON and the player receives the contract's locked `potential_payout`

#### Scenario: Unselected asset touches first → LOST

- **WHEN** a contract selects `VOL` and Asset 1 is the sole first-toucher at tick 8
- **THEN** the contract is LOST and the player receives nothing (the stake paid at placement remains with the house)

#### Scenario: Both assets touch on the same tick → REFUNDED

- **WHEN** both Asset 1 and Asset 2 first satisfy the touch condition on the same tick
- **THEN** the contract is REFUNDED with `settle_reason = "tie"` regardless of the player's selection
- **AND** the stake is credited back via a REFUND ledger row (`amount = +stake`)

#### Scenario: Neither asset touches within T_max → REFUNDED

- **WHEN** the race reaches tick `T_max` with both assets still strictly below the barrier
- **THEN** the contract is REFUNDED with `settle_reason = "timeout"` regardless of the player's selection
- **AND** the stake is credited back via a REFUND ledger row (`amount = +stake`)

### Requirement: First-to-Touch Resolution

The race outcome MUST be determined by discrete-time first-passage against the shared up-barrier `H`. For each asset `i`, the hitting time is:

```
τ_i = inf{t ∈ {1, 2, 3, ...} : S_i(t) ≥ H}
```

with `τ_i = ∞` if the barrier is never reached within `T_max` ticks. The touch condition is `S_i(t) ≥ H` — an exact-barrier value counts as a touch. Comparison MUST be performed at 1e-8 precision, consistent with `rise-fall-settlement`'s spot-vs-barrier comparison discipline.

The four outcomes are mutually exclusive and exhaustive:

| Outcome | Condition | Contract status |
|---|---|---|
| Asset 1 wins | `τ₁ < τ₂` | WON if `selection = DRIFT`, else LOST |
| Asset 2 wins | `τ₂ < τ₁` | WON if `selection = VOL`, else LOST |
| Tie | `τ₁ = τ₂ < ∞` | REFUNDED (`settle_reason = "tie"`) |
| Timeout | `τ₁ = τ₂ = ∞` | REFUNDED (`settle_reason = "timeout"`) |

Prices are observed **only at integer tick boundaries** `t = 1, 2, 3, ...`. The backend MUST NOT apply continuous-time monitoring: an asset whose continuous-time path would have crossed `H` between two tick boundaries but whose observed prices at both boundaries are strictly below `H` has NOT touched. This discrete-monitoring rule is what makes the product's probabilities grid-computed rather than closed-form (per `barrier-race-pricing` "Win Probability Engine").

The race terminates at `settle_tick_index = min(τ₁, τ₂)` when either asset has touched, or at `T_max` on timeout. No ticks after the settling tick are generated, revealed, or persisted.

#### Scenario: Exact-barrier value counts as a touch

- **WHEN** an asset's observed price at tick 5 equals `H` exactly (within 1e-8)
- **THEN** that asset has touched the barrier and `τ_i = 5`

#### Scenario: Intra-tick excursion above the barrier is not a touch

- **WHEN** an asset's observed price is strictly below `H` at tick 5 and strictly below `H` at tick 6
- **THEN** that asset has NOT touched the barrier on tick 5 or tick 6, irrespective of any notional continuous-time path between the two observations

#### Scenario: Race terminates on the first touch

- **WHEN** Asset 1 touches the barrier at tick 7 and Asset 2 is strictly below `H` at tick 7
- **THEN** `settle_tick_index = 7`
- **AND** no tick with index greater than 7 is generated or revealed for this contract

### Requirement: Contract Lifecycle

A contract MUST progress through a four-state status machine:

1. `OPEN` — from `/buy` acceptance until terminal. The full race path is generated at acceptance (per "Race Path Generation and Auditability"), so the outcome is determined at acceptance; the contract stays `OPEN` while the path is progressively revealed to the player tick by tick. Within `OPEN` there are two observable sub-states distinguished by the `race_start_at` column:
   - **Awaiting reveal** (`race_start_at IS NULL`): from `/buy` acceptance until the reveal stream begins.
   - **Racing** (`race_start_at IS NOT NULL`): from the first revealed tick until the settlement trigger fires at `settle_tick_index`.
2. One terminal state: `WON`, `LOST`, or `REFUNDED`.

A contract MUST NOT transition out of a terminal state. The status enum is exactly `{OPEN, WON, LOST, REFUNDED}`.

REFUNDED contracts are distinguished by `settle_reason` (admin-only column per `barrier-race-database` "contracts Table"), which takes one of three values:

- `"tie"` — both assets first satisfied the touch condition on the same tick. An ordinary race outcome. `settle_tick_index` is populated.
- `"timeout"` — neither asset touched within `T_max` ticks. An ordinary race outcome, expected to be vanishingly rare under any config that passes the duration gate in `barrier-race-pricing` "Config Validation Gates". `settle_tick_index = T_max`.
- `"operator_refund"` — an operator or automated monitoring voided the race (for example, a discovered path-generation defect or a config published in error). The only non-outcome refund path. `settle_tick_index` MAY be NULL if the race was voided before resolution.

Because the path is generated in full at acceptance, a process restart does not lose the outcome: recovery re-reads the persisted seed and locked config, replays the path deterministically, and resumes or completes settlement (per `barrier-race-settlement` "Process Lifecycle and Recovery"). There is no process-lifecycle refund reason.

#### Scenario: Reveal begins while OPEN

- **WHEN** a contract is `OPEN` with `race_start_at IS NULL` and the reveal stream emits its first tick
- **THEN** the contract stays `OPEN`
- **AND** `race_start_at` is set to that instant

#### Scenario: OPEN → terminal on settlement trigger

- **WHEN** the reveal reaches `settle_tick_index` for an `OPEN` contract
- **THEN** the contract transitions to one of `WON`, `LOST`, or `REFUNDED` per "Asset Selection"

#### Scenario: Terminal state is final

- **WHEN** a contract has status `WON`, `LOST`, or `REFUNDED`
- **THEN** no subsequent event transitions it to any other status
- **AND** re-running settlement for that contract is a no-op

#### Scenario: Restart replays the path rather than refunding

- **WHEN** the backend restarts while a contract is `OPEN` and the contract's `settle_tick_index` was reached during the downtime window
- **THEN** recovery replays the path from the persisted seed and locked config and settles the contract to its determined outcome
- **AND** the contract is NOT refunded on account of the restart

### Requirement: Contract Timestamps

Every contract MUST carry the following timing fields, timestamps as RFC 3339 values:

| Field | Meaning | NULL when |
|---|---|---|
| `start_time` | The instant the backend received the `/buy` request. The anchor for `timeout_deadline` and the instant at which the race config and probabilities are locked. | never |
| `timeout_deadline` | The latest instant at which the race can still be running, computed as `start_time + T_max × Δt` and stored at INSERT. Bounds the reveal, not the outcome (the outcome is already determined at acceptance). | never |
| `race_start_at` | The instant the reveal stream emitted the contract's first tick. | `OPEN`-while-awaiting-reveal; `operator_refund` voided before reveal began |
| `settle_tick_index` | The integer tick index at which the race resolved: `min(τ₁, τ₂)` for WON / LOST / `tie`, `T_max` for `timeout`. Not a timestamp. | `operator_refund` voided before resolution |
| `settle_tick_at` | The instant the resolving tick was revealed and the contract was settled. | non-terminal; `operator_refund` voided before resolution |

Invariants:

- For every terminal contract other than a pre-resolution `operator_refund`: `start_time ≤ race_start_at < settle_tick_at` and `settle_tick_at ≤ timeout_deadline`.
- `settle_tick_index ≥ 1` for WON, LOST, and `tie` contracts — the earliest possible touch is tick 1.
- For `timeout` REFUNDED contracts: `settle_tick_index = T_max`.
- Unlike `rise-fall-contract`, a REFUNDED Barrier Race contract normally DOES carry a resolving tick: `tie` and `timeout` are outcomes of a completed race, not the absence of a resolution. Only `operator_refund` may leave `settle_tick_index` and `settle_tick_at` NULL.
- `settle_tick_index` is derived from the path and MUST be reproducible by replaying the persisted seed against the locked config. `settle_tick_at` is wall-clock and is NOT reproducible; it records when the reveal reached the resolving tick.
- The relationship between `settle_tick_index` and `settle_tick_at` is not fixed: the reveal cadence is a presentation concern (per `barrier-race-game-ui`) and MAY compress for long races, so `settle_tick_at − race_start_at` need not equal `settle_tick_index × Δt`.

#### Scenario: timeout_deadline set at placement

- **WHEN** a placement is accepted at `start_time = T0` under a config with `T_max = 3000` and `Δt = 1.0`
- **THEN** the row's `timeout_deadline = T0 + 3000 seconds` is computed and stored at INSERT
- **AND** `timeout_deadline` is never updated after INSERT

#### Scenario: Won contract carries a resolving tick index

- **WHEN** a contract settles WON because the selected asset touched alone at tick 7
- **THEN** `settle_tick_index = 7`
- **AND** `settle_tick_at` is non-null and satisfies `race_start_at < settle_tick_at ≤ timeout_deadline`

#### Scenario: Tie refund carries a resolving tick index

- **WHEN** a contract is REFUNDED because both assets touched at tick 5
- **THEN** `settle_reason = "tie"`
- **AND** `settle_tick_index = 5` and `settle_tick_at` is non-null
- **AND** the row is distinguishable from an `operator_refund` by `settle_reason` and by the populated tick fields

#### Scenario: Timeout refund pins the index at T_max

- **WHEN** a contract is REFUNDED because neither asset touched within `T_max = 3000` ticks
- **THEN** `settle_reason = "timeout"`
- **AND** `settle_tick_index = 3000`

#### Scenario: Reveal compression does not break timestamp invariants

- **WHEN** a race resolves at `settle_tick_index = 40` and the reveal cadence is compressed so the whole race is presented in under 15 seconds
- **THEN** `settle_tick_at − race_start_at` is under 15 seconds and need not equal 40 seconds
- **AND** `settle_tick_at ≤ timeout_deadline` still holds

### Requirement: Race Parameter Locking

The complete race configuration MUST be locked at `start_time` and MUST NOT change for the life of the contract. The locked set is:

| Parameter | Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `s0_drift` | S₁,₀ | Asset 1 start price |
| `s0_vol` | S₂,₀ | Asset 2 start price |
| `barrier` | H | Shared up-barrier; first asset to touch or exceed wins |
| `mu_drift` | μ₁ | Asset 1 per-tick drift |
| `mu_vol` | μ₂ | Asset 2 per-tick drift |
| `sigma_drift` | σ₁ | Asset 1 per-tick volatility |
| `sigma_vol` | σ₂ | Asset 2 per-tick volatility |
| `rho` | ρ | Correlation between the two Brownian drivers |
| `dt` | Δt | Tick duration in seconds |
| `t_max` | T_max | Timeout threshold in ticks |
| `commission_rate` | c | House edge added to each implied probability |

The locked set is persisted together with its `config_version` in `locked_pricing` (per `barrier-race-pricing` "Recorded Pricing Inputs"). The barrier is known before placement and is quotable on the `/preview` and `/buy` responses — there is no deferred barrier as in `rise-fall-contract` "Barrier Locking".

Subsequent admin edits to the race configuration MUST NOT re-price, re-generate the path for, or re-resolve any contract that was already accepted. The locked values are authoritative for that contract's lifecycle and for any later audit replay.

#### Scenario: Barrier quotable at placement

- **WHEN** `POST /api/barrier-race/buy` succeeds under a config with `barrier = "102.000000"`
- **THEN** the HTTP response body includes `barrier = "102.000000"`
- **AND** the value is identical to the `barrier` persisted in the contract's locked config

#### Scenario: Admin config change does not affect an open contract

- **WHEN** an admin publishes a new config version changing `sigma_vol` from `0.006` to `0.008` while a contract accepted under the previous version is `OPEN`
- **THEN** the `OPEN` contract retains its locked `sigma_vol = 0.006` and its locked `config_version`
- **AND** the contract's already-generated path and determined outcome are unchanged
- **AND** any new placement after the change is accepted under the new `config_version`

#### Scenario: Locked config drives audit replay

- **WHEN** an auditor replays a settled contract from its persisted seed
- **THEN** the replay uses the contract's locked config values, not the live config
- **AND** the replay reproduces the persisted `settle_tick_index` and outcome

### Requirement: Race Path Generation and Auditability

The backend MUST generate the complete race path at `/buy` acceptance, before responding, and MUST persist enough state to replay it deterministically.

Path generation follows the discrete GBM recursion in log space. With adjusted drift `ν_i = (μ_i − ½σ_i²)Δt` and per-tick standard deviation `s_i = σ_i√Δt`:

```
x_i(t) = x_i(t−1) + ν_i + s_i × Z_i(t)
S_i(t) = exp(x_i(t))
```

where `x_i(0) = ln(S_i,0)`. The paired shocks MUST be correlated by Cholesky decomposition:

```
Z₁ ~ N(0,1),  X ~ N(0,1),  independent
Z₂ = ρ × Z₁ + √(1 − ρ²) × X
```

Requirements on the generator:

- The seed MUST be drawn from a cryptographically secure random source. Non-cryptographic PRNGs (`math/rand` without a CSPRNG seed, `Math.random`) MUST NOT be used to generate a path that determines money movement.
- The seed MUST be persisted on the contract row as an admin-only column (per `barrier-race-database` "contracts Table"). It MUST NOT be exposed on player-facing endpoints or WebSocket envelopes before the contract is terminal, because the seed reveals the outcome.
- The pair `(seed, locked config)` MUST fully determine the path. Replaying it MUST reproduce every observed price, both hitting times, `settle_tick_index`, and the outcome, bit-for-bit.
- The path MUST NOT be regenerated, extended, or modified after acceptance. The path a contract settles against is the path generated at acceptance.
- Generation MUST stop at the resolving tick: no prices beyond `settle_tick_index` are generated or persisted.

Only the shock draws use randomness. `ν_i`, `s_i`, and `ln(H)` are deterministic functions of the locked config and MUST be derived, not stored as independent inputs.

#### Scenario: Path generated before the buy response

- **WHEN** `POST /api/barrier-race/buy` is accepted
- **THEN** the full path, both hitting times, `settle_tick_index`, and the outcome are computed and persisted before the HTTP response is written
- **AND** no later event can change them

#### Scenario: Replay reproduces the outcome

- **WHEN** an auditor replays a settled contract's `(seed, locked config)`
- **THEN** the regenerated price series matches the persisted series exactly
- **AND** the regenerated `settle_tick_index` and outcome match the persisted values

#### Scenario: Correlated shocks honour rho

- **WHEN** a config with `rho = -0.5` is used to generate a large number of shock pairs
- **THEN** the sample correlation of the pairs converges to `-0.5`
- **AND** each marginal shock series is standard normal

#### Scenario: Seed not exposed while OPEN

- **WHEN** a player reads their own `OPEN` contract via `GET /api/barrier-race/contracts/{id}`
- **THEN** the response does NOT include the seed
- **AND** the response does NOT include any path tick beyond those already revealed

#### Scenario: Weak randomness rejected

- **WHEN** a path generator is implemented against a non-cryptographic PRNG or a caller-supplied seed
- **THEN** the implementation violates this requirement and MUST NOT be deployed

### Requirement: Race Timeout Bound

The race MUST be bounded at `T_max` ticks. If both assets are strictly below `H` at tick `T_max`, the contract MUST be settled as REFUNDED with `settle_reason = "timeout"` and the stake credited back in full.

`T_max` is a per-config parameter, locked at `start_time`. Its default is `3000` ticks. It is a safety bound rather than a live product parameter: any config that passes the duration gate in `barrier-race-pricing` "Config Validation Gates" has timeout probability below 0.1%, so timeout refunds are expected to be vanishingly rare in production.

`T_max` is not a player-selectable duration. Barrier Race has no `duration` field on the wire; the race length is an outcome of the path, not an input. This is the structural difference from `rise-fall-contract` "Duration Bounds".

#### Scenario: Timeout refunds the stake

- **WHEN** a race reaches tick `T_max` with both assets strictly below `H`
- **THEN** the contract is REFUNDED with `settle_reason = "timeout"`
- **AND** the stake is credited back via one REFUND ledger row (`amount = +stake`; `buy_price` on the contract row equals the stake)
- **AND** `sell_price = buy_price` per `barrier-race-pricing` "Settlement Payout Computation"

#### Scenario: No duration on the wire

- **WHEN** a placement request carries a `duration` field
- **THEN** the field is not part of the request contract and MUST NOT influence the race
- **AND** the race length is determined solely by the generated path and `T_max`

#### Scenario: T_max locked per contract

- **WHEN** an admin changes `t_max` from `3000` to `5000` while a contract accepted under the old value is `OPEN`
- **THEN** the `OPEN` contract's timeout bound remains `3000` ticks
- **AND** its `timeout_deadline` computed at INSERT is unchanged

### Requirement: Race Configuration Bounds

The race configuration MUST be validated at admin-write time, before the row lands. A write failing any of the following is rejected with `invalid_setting_value` (per `barrier-race-risk` "Per-Config Risk Parameters"):

| Constraint | Rationale |
|---|---|
| `barrier > max(s0_drift, s0_vol)` | The barrier is an up-barrier; a barrier at or below a start price would resolve the race at tick 0 |
| `s0_drift > 0` and `s0_vol > 0` | Log-space dynamics require strictly positive prices |
| `sigma_drift > 0` and `sigma_vol > 0` | A zero-volatility asset is deterministic and breaks the grid kernel |
| `rho ∈ (−1, 1)` | Cholesky requires `√(1 − ρ²) > 0`; `\|ρ\| = 1` degenerates the bivariate normal |
| `dt > 0` | Per-tick parameters are scaled by `Δt` |
| `t_max ≥ 1` | The earliest possible touch is tick 1 |
| `commission_rate ≥ 0` | A negative commission would price above fair odds |
| `min_stake > 0` and `min_stake ≤ max_stake` | Stake bounds must describe a non-empty range |

These structural bounds are necessary but not sufficient. A config that passes them MUST additionally pass the probability, viability, and duration gates in `barrier-race-pricing` ("Offered-Odds Viability" and "Config Validation Gates") before it can be published for play.

At `/buy` time the risk cascade validates the request against the live config's stake bounds only. Because admin writes are structurally bounded and gated, no re-validation of the structural constraints is needed per request.

#### Scenario: Barrier at or below start price rejected

- **WHEN** an admin writes `barrier = "100.000000"` with `s0_drift = "100.000000"`
- **THEN** the write is rejected with `invalid_setting_value`

#### Scenario: Degenerate correlation rejected

- **WHEN** an admin writes `rho = -1.0` or `rho = 1.0`
- **THEN** the write is rejected with `invalid_setting_value`

#### Scenario: Zero volatility rejected

- **WHEN** an admin writes `sigma_vol = 0.0`
- **THEN** the write is rejected with `invalid_setting_value`

#### Scenario: Structurally valid config still requires the pricing gates

- **WHEN** an admin writes a config that satisfies every structural constraint above but whose computed `P_vol + c` exceeds `1 − 1e-6`
- **THEN** the config is rejected before publication per `barrier-race-pricing` "Offered-Odds Viability"

### Requirement: Stake Bounds

The `stake` (USDT) on the wire MUST be a 6-decimal-place decimal string whose value is strictly positive and falls within `[min_stake, max_stake]` for the active race configuration. The bounds are read from live config settings (per `barrier-race-risk` "Per-Config Risk Parameters").

The stake is locked at placement and debited verbatim. The contract row stores `stake` in `parameters.stake` (JSONB), and stores the same value as the top-level NUMERIC column `buy_price` (per `barrier-race-database` "contracts Table" and `barrier-race-pricing` "Commission Model").

Monetary arithmetic MUST use a decimal-safe representation (`NUMERIC(20,6)` in Postgres, `shopspring/decimal` in Go). Whole-credit or `float64` money handling is forbidden.

#### Scenario: Zero or negative stake rejected

- **WHEN** a placement carries `stake = "0.000000"` or any negative value
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `INVALID_STAKE`

#### Scenario: Stake below configured minimum

- **WHEN** the placement's stake is below the config's `min_stake`
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `INVALID_STAKE` (specifically `stake_below_min`)

#### Scenario: Stake above configured maximum

- **WHEN** the placement's stake is above the config's `max_stake`
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `INVALID_STAKE` (specifically `stake_above_max`)

#### Scenario: Stake stored verbatim in two places

- **WHEN** a placement with `stake = "10.000000"` is accepted
- **THEN** the contract row's `parameters.stake` is the exact string `"10.000000"`
- **AND** the row's `buy_price` NUMERIC column equals `10.000000`
- **AND** the PLAY_DEBIT ledger row has `amount = -10.000000`

### Requirement: One Active Race Per Account

The backend MUST allow an account to hold at most **one** non-terminal Barrier Race contract at a time. A placement submitted while the account already holds a non-terminal contract MUST be rejected with `race_in_progress`.

This inverts `rise-fall-contract` "Concurrent Positions Allowed". A Barrier Race is a single progressively-revealed race with its own reveal cadence, and the product presents exactly one race at a time; allowing concurrent races would make the reveal surface ambiguous and would let a player hold correlated exposure across paths generated from independent seeds.

The constraint MUST be enforced atomically against the account's contract set so that two placements racing in parallel cannot both be accepted.

#### Scenario: Second concurrent placement rejected

- **WHEN** an account holds an `OPEN` contract and submits a new placement
- **THEN** the placement is rejected with `race_in_progress`
- **AND** no stake is debited and no path is generated

#### Scenario: Placement accepted once the prior race is terminal

- **WHEN** an account's only contract reaches `WON`, `LOST`, or `REFUNDED` and the account then submits a new placement
- **THEN** the placement is evaluated normally against the remaining risk cascade

#### Scenario: Parallel placements cannot both win

- **WHEN** an account submits two placements simultaneously with distinct idempotency keys and holds no non-terminal contract
- **THEN** exactly one is accepted and the other is rejected with `race_in_progress`

#### Scenario: Retry of the same idempotency key is not a second race

- **WHEN** a client retries an accepted placement with the same idempotency key while that contract is `OPEN`
- **THEN** the original contract is returned per `barrier-race-api` "Idempotency"
- **AND** the retry is NOT rejected with `race_in_progress`

### Requirement: Backend-Owned Trading Logic

All trading logic MUST live on the backend, identically for real-money (`ACC-`) and demo (`DEMO-`) accounts:

- Contracts are created exclusively by the backend in response to `POST /api/barrier-race/buy`. There is no WebSocket placement path.
- The race path is generated by the backend (see "Race Path Generation and Auditability"). The frontend MUST NOT generate, extend, or simulate a path. The client-side path generation used in prototypes is not a permitted production path.
- The win / loss / refund outcome is decided by the backend (see "Asset Selection" and `barrier-race-settlement`).
- Balance debits and credits happen on the backend; the frontend reads via `GET /api/barrier-race/balance` and `BALANCE_UPDATE`.
- Contract history is sourced via `GET /api/barrier-race/contracts`.

Because the outcome is determined at acceptance rather than by later external events, the reveal surface carries a disclosure obligation the rise-fall product does not have: the backend MUST NOT expose any part of the path ahead of the reveal position, and MUST NOT expose the seed or the outcome, until the contract is terminal. A client that could read ahead could act on a known outcome.

The reveal cadence is a presentation concern owned by `barrier-race-game-ui`. It MAY compress for long races. It MUST NOT alter `settle_tick_index`, the outcome, or any money movement.

#### Scenario: No client-side bypass

- **WHEN** the frontend handles a placement gesture in any mode (real, demo, or embed)
- **THEN** there is no code path that generates a race path, decides an outcome, or moves a balance without a corresponding backend response

#### Scenario: Unrevealed path not readable

- **WHEN** a contract is `OPEN` with the reveal at tick 3 of a race resolving at tick 9
- **THEN** no player-facing endpoint or WebSocket envelope exposes prices for ticks 4 through 9
- **AND** no player-facing surface exposes the seed, `settle_tick_index`, or the outcome

#### Scenario: Full path readable once terminal

- **WHEN** a contract has settled
- **THEN** the full revealed price series, `settle_tick_index`, and the outcome are readable by the owning player
- **AND** the seed remains admin-only

#### Scenario: Reveal compression does not change money

- **WHEN** the reveal cadence for a long race is compressed to keep total reveal time within the presentation budget
- **THEN** `settle_tick_index`, the outcome, `potential_payout`, and every ledger row are identical to what an uncompressed reveal would produce

#### Scenario: Demo accounts follow the identical path

- **WHEN** a `DEMO-` account places a race
- **THEN** path generation, outcome determination, and settlement follow the identical backend code path as an `ACC-` account
- **AND** only the account's funding source differs
